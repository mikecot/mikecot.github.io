# Token Burn Analytics Platform

Deflationary mechanism dashboard for LAVA token economics and supply reduction.

![Burn Dashboard Main View](/assets/images/blockchain-infrastructure/burn/burn-dashboard-main.png)

**Features:** Real-time burn tracking, supply dynamics, value metrics, economic modeling

![Detailed Analytics View](/assets/images/blockchain-infrastructure/burn/burn-analytics-detailed.png)

## Economic Modeling Platform

The Lava Burn platform provides real-time analytics and economic modeling for the LAVA token's deflationary mechanisms. Built to track supply reduction and analyze the economic impact of token burns on the overall ecosystem.

## Key Analytics

- **Real-time Burn Tracking**: Live monitoring of token burn events and rates
- **Supply Dynamics**: Advanced modeling of supply reduction impacts
- **Value Metrics**: Economic analysis of burn effects on token value
- **Stakeholder Impact**: Analytics for community and investor insights

## Deflationary Impact

With over 50M tokens burned and a 15% total supply reduction, the platform tracks significant deflationary pressure while serving 150k+ active stakeholders with detailed economic analytics.

**Link**: [burn.lavanet.xyz](https://burn.lavanet.xyz/)

**Stack:** Next.js, TypeScript, Python, Material-UI, Vercel
